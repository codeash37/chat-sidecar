# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- The pane, its sections, the plan band and connector detection are rewritten from a behavior spec (`SPEC.md`); features, settings, commands and tools are unchanged.
- Connector logos come from Simple Icons (CC0); a service without one shows a letter badge.

## 0.4.0

- Tasks are answered in the **Tasks for me** section; the separate My tasks pane is gone.
- **Answered** lists the session's closed tasks; **Elsewhere** shows its count when folded.
- The plan band shows `● N for you` while tasks wait.
- **Bar style** setting: gradient, segments, line, dots, pixel.
- **Git** section: base branch, worktree, main repo.
- **Background** lists every watched port, warns when another process holds one, and shows details on press.
- The pane shows its title. Subagent groups: only Running is filled.

## 0.3.0

- Settings screen: sections on/off and order, Background ports, plan band colour.
- Task system (from human-in-the-loop) built in; Elsewhere preview.
- Renamed from chat-pane to chat-sidecar.

## 0.2.0

- Background section: servers on watched ports and test runs with progress.

## 0.1.0

- First version: To-do, Subagents, Connectors, Context files, Skills, plan band.
